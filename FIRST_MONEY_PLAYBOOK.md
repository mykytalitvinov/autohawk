# AUTOHAWK First Money Playbook

Goal: get the first paid customer before trying to sell the software or buy inventory.

## Positioning

Do not sell it as "AI app" first. Sell the result:

"I help young drivers in Germany find a safe first car faster. I scan fresh listings, filter risky engines/scams, compare market price, and send you only the best candidates with a simple explanation."

German one-liner:

"Ich finde fuer dich ein gutes erstes Auto in Deutschland: frisch inseriert, fairer Preis, wenig Risiko, mit einfacher Erklaerung und Checkliste."

## First offer

### Starter - 49 EUR
- Budget/use-case interview.
- 3-5 checked listings.
- Simple risk explanation.
- Questions to ask seller.
- No guarantee, no inspection.

### Serious Buyer - 99 EUR
- Everything in Starter.
- 24-48h monitoring.
- Up to 10 candidates.
- Message templates in German.
- Ranking: best/safe/avoid.

### Premium Local Help - 199 EUR+
- Only later.
- Phone support while visiting.
- Checklist for test drive.
- Do not promise mechanical certainty.

## What not to do yet

- Do not buy cars with your own money first.
- Do not promise profit.
- Do not promise accident-free certainty.
- Do not act like a dealer unless the legal setup is clear.
- Do not sell cars as a business before understanding Gewaehrleistung/warranty risk.

## Customer profile

Best first customers:
- Russian/Ukrainian-speaking people in Germany.
- Young drivers getting first car.
- Students/Azubis.
- People with 1000-5000 EUR budget.
- People scared of scams/repairs.

Avoid first:
- People wanting luxury cars cheap.
- People expecting guarantee.
- People who want you to buy the car for them.
- People arguing over 20 EUR.

## Workflow for one paid customer

1. Ask budget, city/radius, manual/automatic, fuel, purpose, max insurance pain, must-have features.
2. Run AUTOHAWK for 2-24h.
3. Export top CHECK/HOT candidates.
4. Manually open each listing.
5. Reject anything with no TUV, bad engine symptoms, accident wording, rust holes, seller weirdness.
6. Deliver a short report:
   - Best option
   - Safe option
   - Cheap option
   - Avoid list
   - Seller questions
   - Inspection checklist

## Product truth

AUTOHAWK is not ready as a SaaS yet. It is already useful as an internal tool for a human-assisted car finding service. First money should come from service, not software subscription.
